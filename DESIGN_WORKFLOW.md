# Website design preference

Before implementing a new website or a substantial redesign, generate three distinctly styled concept images for the user to choose the main direction. Wait for the user's selection before building the full website.

## Hair2 direction brief

- Opening hero: two coordinated photos of the same adult woman turning or dancing, one facing forward and one facing away with her hair swooshing.
- Scroll reveal: large covering dots shrink progressively as the user scrolls, until the full photos are clear. Provide reduced-motion support when implemented.
- Six service items presented through minimal object photography: scissors, color brush, styling product, comb, treatment texture, hairpins. Include descriptions, timeframes and prices.
- Reference materials: ivory and gray grounds, black salon tools, silver palette knives with product textures, antique silver tray holding a crisp hair menu.
- Current stage: approved bold fashion direction; implementation authorized.
